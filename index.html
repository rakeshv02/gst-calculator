Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Australian GST rate?
Australia's GST rate is 10%. It applies to most goods and services sold in Australia.
How do I remove GST?
Divide the GST-inclusive amount by 1.1 (for 10% GST) to get the original amount. Our calculator does this automatically.
