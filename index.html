What is the GST Calculator?

A GST calculator adds or removes Goods and Services Tax from a price. This free GST calculator is set up for Australia's 10% rate, letting you find the GST amount, the GST-inclusive total, or strip GST out of a tax-inclusive figure in seconds.

How it works

To add GST, multiply the net (GST-exclusive) price by the rate: GST = net × 0.10, and the total is net × 1.10.

To remove GST from a tax-inclusive amount, divide by 1.1: net = inclusive ÷ 1.1, and the GST portion is inclusive − net (equivalently inclusive ÷ 11).

Adding GSTA product costs $200 before tax. GST = $200 × 0.10 = $20, so the GST-inclusive price is $220.
Removing GSTAn invoice total is $330 including GST. Net = $330 ÷ 1.1 = $300, so the GST included is $30.

When to use it

  • Preparing quotes and invoices that must show GST.
  • Checking how much GST is included in a receipt.
  • Pricing products so the shelf price includes tax.
  • Completing a Business Activity Statement (BAS).

Is the GST amount just 10% of the total price?

No — on a GST-inclusive total the tax is one-eleventh of the total, not 10% of it. For $110 inclusive, the GST is $10 (110 ÷ 11), not $11.
